02   Introduction

Most people overcomplicate UGC.

They think they need 100k followers, professional cameras, viral content, and a huge personal brand. But UGC is fundamentally different from influencer marketing.

Brands are not paying you for your audience. They're paying you for your ability to create content that feels authentic and converts.

That's why UGC is one of the easiest online business models to start right now. You can literally start with:

📱

Your Phone

No fancy camera required. Your phone shoots better than most cameras from 5 years ago.

🏠

Products At Home

Skincare, tech, clothing — you already own your first portfolio pieces.

🆓

Free Software

📁

A Simple Portfolio

03   What Is UGC?

What Is UGC?

UGC stands for User Generated Content. It's content created by normal people that brands use across their entire marketing stack — not just social media.

TikTok Ads Instagram Reels Product Pages Websites Email Campaigns Paid Advertising

UGC works because it feels like a recommendation instead of a commercial. That's why brands are moving away from polished ads and toward creators who can make relatable content.

2 Types of UGC

Type 01

Organic UGC (AKA Canvas UGC)

  • Focused on virality & awareness
  • Posted on social media
  • Great for beginners
  • Easier to start with

Type 02

Paid Ads UGC

  • Used in paid advertisements
  • Brands pay more for usage rights
  • Focused on sales & conversions
  • More scalable long-term

You don't need clients to start. Create demo videos using products you already own — skincare, tech, apps, fashion. Your goal is a portfolio before getting paid deals.

06   Your First Videos

Your First 6 Videos

Choose 6 products you already use. Think: water bottle, protein powder, skincare product, coffee machine, productivity app, editing software, or a clothing brand.

Step 01 — Study Viral UGC

  • Search the product on TikTok & Reels
  • Study hooks that grab attention
  • Note the editing style & pacing
  • Analyze storytelling patterns
  • Map out the CTA structure

Step 02 — Create Your Version

  • "Steal like an artist"
  • Don't reinvent — remake in your style

  • These become your proof & positioning
  • Film in good lighting, clean audio
Your first 6 videos should be your BEST work. They become your portfolio, your proof, and your positioning when pitching brands.
07   What Makes Good UGC

Spend 90% Of Your Time On The Hook

Most beginners obsess over editing, transitions, and production quality. That's backwards. The rest of your video doesn't matter if nobody watches past the first three seconds.

People decide whether to keep watching in under 3 seconds. If your hook doesn't stop the scroll, the rest of your video is invisible — no matter how good it is.

The fastest way to improve your content isn't spending more time editing. It's writing, rewriting, and testing your hook until it's impossible to scroll past. Spend 90% of your creative energy there. The video itself will follow.

The Curiosity Loop

The most effective UGC creators don't just write good hooks — they use a storytelling technique called the curiosity loop. It's the secret behind why you've ever watched a video longer than you planned to.

A curiosity loop raises a question in the viewer's mind — then withholds the answer just long enough to make them stay. The brain is wired to seek resolution. Once a question is opened, it physically wants to close it.

Every great UGC video opens a loop in the first three seconds and closes it at the end. The viewer watches the whole thing without realising they were sold to.

How The Loop Works

❓

Open the loop

Your hook raises a question the viewer immediately wants answered. It creates tension.

↓
⏳

Build the tension

You tease the answer — without giving it away. The viewer stays because the loop is still open.

↓
✓

Close the loop

The payoff. You resolve the question — and naturally, the product is the answer.

Hook Templates That Open A Loop

"I went from [x] to [x] within [timeframe] without [common belief]"
"If I had 30 minutes to [desired outcome], I wouldn't [common belief]"
"If you're [target audience] trying to achieve [desired outcome], I wouldn't [common belief] — let me explain"
"[Timeframe] days ago I [achieved desired outcome] without [common belief]"

Each template works because it challenges something the viewer already believes — that's the question it plants. Wait, you did that WITHOUT doing the thing everyone says you need? The loop is open. They have to watch to find out how.

09   Portfolio

Your Portfolio Doesn't Need To Be Fancy

Most beginners think they need a website, professional branding, and 100 videos. You don't. What you need is a portfolio that makes a brand want to keep reading within the first five seconds of opening it.

Your portfolio has a hook too. The opening should immediately give the brand a reason to care — not with a nice bio, but with your results. Lead with your content metrics and let the numbers do the talking before they've even seen a single video.

Winning Portfolio Structure

1

Hook — Lead With Your Metrics

Open with your strongest content numbers. Views, watch time, engagement rate, click-through rate — whatever proves your content performs. This gives the brand an immediate reason to keep reading. Even as a beginner, your demo videos will have data. Use it.

2

Introduction — Who You Are

A brief, confident write-up about yourself — your niche, your style, and why you create. This isn't a life story. Two or three sentences that tell the brand exactly who they're working with.

3

Best Video Examples

Your 4–6 strongest UGC videos. Only your best work — a tight portfolio with four great videos beats a bloated one with twenty average ones every time.

4

Testimonials + Contact CTA

Social proof from any brands or collaborators you've worked with, followed by a clear, single call-to-action. Tell them exactly how to reach you and what to say.

10   Finding Brands

Where To Find UGC Deals

The easiest brands to pitch are brands already using UGC — because they already understand the value of creator content.

01

Instagram DMs

Find brands running UGC, DM them directly, ask for the marketing contact, and send your portfolio.

02

Meta Ads Library

Search brands currently running ads. If they're spending money on ads, they're spending money on content.

03

Creator Platforms

Cohley, Vidsy, Billo, Insense, Trend — these platforms connect brands with UGC creators directly.

04

Creator Referrals

Build relationships with other UGC creators in your space and ask them to refer you to brands they've worked with. Creators pass on overflow work all the time — being in that circle puts you first in line.

11   Common Mistakes

Avoid These Mistakes

✕

Waiting to feel ready. You learn UGC by doing — not by endlessly watching tutorials. Ship your first video this week.

✕

Trying to be perfect. Your first videos won't be perfect. That's completely normal and expected.

✕

Posting random content. Everything you put on your profile should support your positioning as a UGC creator.

✕

Not reaching out. Most beginners never get clients because they never pitch. Outreach is the job.

✕

Overcomplicating gear. Your phone is enough. Good scripting and storytelling matter far more than expensive equipment.
